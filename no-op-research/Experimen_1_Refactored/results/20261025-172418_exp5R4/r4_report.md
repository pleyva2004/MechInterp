# Experiment 5, R4: own and wrong across layers (Mapper on the capital-centred stream at T)

Run `20261025-172418_exp5R4`. Data `results/20261025-170021_exp5res` (100 rows per filler type). Pre-registration: `PREDICTIONS.md` (R4-a, R4-b) and the operational addendum `PREDICTIONS_R4.md` (both copied here; the addendum was written before this script touched the full data). Code `exp5/r4_flow.py`.

**Points.** Per filler type and τ: x_l(T), l = 1..12, of the own and wrong sequence of each (row, cell), LayerNorm-normalised without gain, then minus the mean of its own/wrong pair (each pair becomes ±δ/2). 2200 points per layer, 26400 per cloud. PCA to 50 (pooled over layers, uncentred because the cloud's mean is 0). **Mapper:** lens = layer; windows of w layers with step s; average linkage per window cut at ε = m × the window's median pairwise distance. Main setting w = 2, s = 1, m = 1 (11 windows: layers 1–2, 2–3, …, 11–12). A node with ≥ 5 points is pure if its own share is ≥ 0.8 or ≤ 0.2. l* = the first layer of the earliest window from which every window is clean through the last window (layers 11–12).

## Summary (hand-written after the run; everything below this section is generated by `exp5/r4_flow.py`)

**Verdicts (pre-registered rule, main setting): R4-a FAIL, R4-b FAIL, both stable.**
- R4-a holds in 0 of 20 grid settings and in 0 of 100 × 20 bootstrap graphs.
- Main setting: l* exists only for rare τ = 1 (l* = 1), and there in 52 of 100 resamples. Common τ = 16, common τ = 1
  and rare τ = 16 have no l* in any setting or resample.
- R4-b fails because R4-a fails.

**Where it fails: only in the windows that contain the last layers.**
- At the main setting, windows 1–2 … 10–11 are clean in all four scored graphs: 0 mixed points, 95–100% of points in
  pure nodes, the rest in nodes under 5 points.
- The single unclean window is 11–12, in common τ = 16, common τ = 1 and rare τ = 16. In each it holds one large mixed
  node with about 2,700 points, 70–79% of them from layer 12, whose layer-12 points have an own share of 0.50–0.51.
- Every failing grid setting fails in windows touching layers 9–12 (all unclean windows are listed in
  `posthoc_checks.txt`). At m = 1.2 the unclean windows start at layer 8, 9 or 10. Rare τ = 1 with w = 3 or 4 also has
  an unclean early window (2–5 or 3–5), where δ roughly doubles between x_3 and x_4.

**Post-hoc checks** (`posthoc_checks.py` / `.txt`, exploratory, written after seeing the result). Why the last window
mixes:
1. **Scale mismatch inside the window.** After LayerNorm normalisation, the RMS radius |δ/2| at x_12 is 0.44–0.52 of
   that at x_11, in all six (filler, τ) clouds. For example, common τ = 16 goes 2.40 → 1.94 → 0.99 over x_10..x_12.
   - A window that pools x_11 and x_12 sets its cut from the larger x_11 spread, so the x_12 own and wrong points
     fall into one node.
   - Clustered alone with the same rule (a 1-layer window), x_12 has 0 mixed points at m ≤ 1.1 in three graphs.
     Common τ = 1 is the exception: x_12 alone has a mixed node at m ≥ 1.0 (0.76 of its points). x_11 alone is
     clean at m ≤ 1.0 everywhere.
   - The pre-declared per-layer rescaled variant (each layer divided by its RMS radius) removes the failure:
     - main setting: R4-a and R4-b hold, with l* = 1 in all six graphs, τ = 31 included;
     - grid: 14 of 20 settings;
     - bootstrap at the main setting: 86 of 100 resamples (100 of 100 at m = 0.8 and 0.9).
   - It fails at m ≥ 1.1 with w = 2 or 3, and at m = 1.2 with every cover.
2. **Nothing is overwritten in the linear sense.** Leave-one-cell-out nearest-centroid accuracy is ≥ 0.998 at every
   layer 1–12 in all four scored graphs. This classifies a pair by the sign of δ · (mean δ of the other 10 cells).
   So a direction shared across capitals separates own from wrong at every layer, x_12 included.
3. **The late cloud fragments rather than merges.** From about x_7–x_9 (τ = 16 and 1), the per-layer 2-cluster
   split drops to purity 0.50. But its minority cluster holds ≤ 1% of the 2200 points (1–12 points at the layers
   checked, x_8, x_10, x_12): average linkage's top split isolates outliers instead of splitting by condition.
   - In Mapper, the late windows break into dozens to hundreds of nodes. Each is pure and holds a median of 4–5 of
     the 11 capitals.
   - At coarse scale (m = 1.2) the late windows, and even single late layers, become mixed. Late own/wrong
     separation is therefore local, not two global blobs.
   - At τ = 31 (adjacent country) the 2-cluster split stays pure through x_11 (through x_12 for common filler).

**The flow graph in plain words** (main setting, `figures/mapper_main.png`).
- **Split at once.** The own and wrong states split into two branches at the first window (layers 1–2, right after
  block 0). Each branch is mostly one node per window, holding all 11 capitals.
- **Two clean strands to mid-depth.** They stay two clean strands to about layers 6–8 (about layer 10 at τ = 31).
- **Fray, don't cross.** From there each strand frays into many small pure nodes. No edge joins a pure-own and a
  pure-wrong node anywhere.
- **The only merge is the last window**, where both branches feed one mixed node made mostly of layer-12 states.
  Rare τ = 1 has no such node.
- **Not explained by single capitals.** Label nulls: swapping own/wrong for random sets of cells leaves 7–8% of
  points in pure nodes, against 92–99% for the real labels. So the separation is a direction shared by all capitals,
  not 22 (capital, condition) groups.

**What this supports.**
- The match difference appears after block 0 and is not linearly overwritten through x_12. That is consistent with
  the intent of R4-a and R4-b, and descriptively l* = 1 ≤ 4.
- But the pre-registered Mapper test fails, stably, because of the final layer. There the normalised own/wrong
  difference halves, and the window pools it with layer 11. At coarse scales the late layers also mix.
- I do not re-score the hypotheses on the rescaled variant: it is a sensitivity check, not the pre-registered test.

**Caveats.**
- The same 11 pairs and one fixed derangement throughout; the bootstrap resamples rows only.
- δ also changes the country's identity, but the cell-flip null argues against identity-only separation.
- Mapper's outcome turns on m and on how windows pool layers of different scale.
- PCA-50 keeps only 0.49–0.62 of x_12's squared norm, though PCA-768 fails in the same window.
- l* is read on window starts.
- The post-hoc checks were chosen after seeing the failure.

## Caveats

- The same 11 country–capital pairs throughout, and the derangement is fixed: a result means consistent across rows and these pairs, not general. The bootstrap resamples rows only.
- δ = own − wrong changes two things at τ: the match and the country's identity (country k vs the country whose deranged capital is k). Purity alone cannot say which one separates the points; the cell-flip null and the cells-per-node numbers above address it.
- Mapper depends on the cover and the clustering scale. The verdict is the main setting; the 20-setting grid, the global-scale rule, the PCA dimension and the bootstrap show how much it moves. Smaller m gives finer, more often pure nodes.
- R4-a is strict: one mixed node of ≥ 5 points in any later window breaks it. The point shares per window show how close a failing graph is.
- Windows overlap, so l* is defined on window starts; with w = 2 a clean window starting at l covers layers l and l + 1.
- Layer l means the stream state x_l: before block l (after blocks 0..l−1); x_12 is after block 11, before ln_final.
- The graph layout (spring layout, x = node mean layer) is for display only.
