## Caveats

- The graphs are mean attention over 2,200 sequences per condition. Their topology is that of the average, not the typical topology of one sequence's attention.
- Own, wrong and random differ only in node 32's 32 edges (checked: identical query rows 0..31). The topological contrasts therefore re-express how the capital's query row attaches to a fixed graph. They add whether a strengthened edge closes a loop or only tightens node 32's attachment.
- The split-half scale compares two 100-row means that differ in all 528 edges (sampling noise everywhere). Own vs wrong differ only in node 32's 32 edges. Split-half is therefore far larger than the swap null and is not a usable null for own vs wrong; as pre-registered, it is context only.
- The persistence thresholds are a pre-registered judgement call. A cycle's representative is the shortest path at birth, which is one valid choice; 'uses the edge {32, τ}' is a property of that representative.
- noBOS keeps raw weights, so late-layer graphs live at small weights (most mass is on BOS). withBOS is dominated by the BOS cone, which fills cycles.
- The swap null tests exchangeability of own and wrong row graphs given these 11 fixed pairs; countries are not resampled.

## Files

- `graphs.npz`: mean graphs [τ, unit, q, k] per filler and condition (control is τ-independent but repeated per τ), with `units` and `taus`.
- `diagrams.npz`: H0 / H1 diagrams per filler, τ, condition, unit and variant (1 − w units).
- `tda_arrays.npz`: observed distances [pair, unit, variant, (W1, bottleneck)], swap nulls [pair, swap, unit, variant], p, null95, split-half [split, unit, variant], transplant, bootstrap MPC frequencies [condition, unit, variant] and E1 role frequencies.
- `tda4.json`: per-graph summaries (E1 role, persistent H1 count, MPC with its cycle) and edge weights with CIs.
- `figures/distances_noBOS.png`, `figures/distances_withBOS.png`: own vs wrong D per unit and τ against the null.
- `figures/mpc_stability.png`: bootstrap frequency of an MPC per unit × τ for the four conditions.
- `figures/graph_*.png`: arc drawing of one unit's noBOS graph (own vs wrong) with its MPC, plus diagrams.
