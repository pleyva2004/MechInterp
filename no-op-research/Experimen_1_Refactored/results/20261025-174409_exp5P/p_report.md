## Interpretation and verification (hand-written after the run; not generated by `exp5/patch.py`)

**Verification.** An independent agent re-implemented every run with plain Hugging Face `GPT2Model` (eager attention;
a forward pre-hook on `h[l]` for the stream, a pre-hook on `attn.c_proj` for the head slices) on rows 0–1 of both
fillers at both τ. All 35 runs matched the saved TL values to ≤ 2.9e-6, and F, F_rev, G, ρ and the bootstrap CIs to
≤ 6e-6. This confirms that the patches hit the intended site, which the identity, l = 0 and causal-locality checks
cannot show by themselves (they pass by construction or catch only a too-early patch). The audit of `exp5/patch.py`
found no bugs. Its script is `scratchpad/exp5_dev/P/verify/hf_verify.py` (prints only). The Caveats line "no HF
cross-check of the patched runs was made here" below is superseded by this check.

**What the patching shows.**
- **The unpatched effect reproduces Exp 4b.** own − wrong late BOS is −0.029 / −0.026 at d = 16 and −0.040 / −0.033
  at d = 31, with ratios to Exp 4b of 0.99–1.01. So pairing by capital gives the same size of effect as pairing by
  country.
- **P-a PASS: late resting is decided by the final token's stream, and the information reaches it in layers 3–7.**
  - Patching x_l(T) for l ≥ 8 moves the whole effect on layers l–11: F = 0.97–1.06. The keys at τ..T−1, which still
    differ, then contribute nothing measurable.
  - Earlier, F is 0.12–0.37 for l = 1–3. F(1) = 0.16–0.37, so the stream after layer 0 already carries a sixth to a
    third of the effect.
  - F jumps at l = 4 (after layer 3, to 0.41–0.62) and climbs through l = 7 (0.86–0.94).
  - The reverse direction gives nearly the same curve (|F − F_rev| ≤ 0.14). That is consistent with additivity
    between T's stream and the context: F ≈ F_rev is necessary for additivity, not sufficient. The addendum's word
    "indicates" overstates this.
- **Part of P-a's ρ > 0 is built in.** For l ≥ 7, more layers have already written into T and fewer measured layers
  remain where the context keys can act. On the informative range l = 1–6, where the layer window stays 6–11, F is not
  monotone: it dips at l = 2–3 in common and is flat over l = 1–3 in rare. Exploratory, not pre-registered: the point
  Spearman over l = 1..6 is 0.77 (common, both τ) and 0.94 (rare, both τ).
- **P-b FAIL, narrowly, in common only.** The four heads' writes at T carry 0.180 [0.136, 0.227] of the late BOS
  effect in common and 0.359 [0.326, 0.392] in rare at d = 16. Common misses the 0.20 threshold on the point estimate,
  but its CI includes 0.20.
  - At d = 31 the transfer is larger: 0.353 / 0.621. Most of the increase comes from L3H0 (0.20 / 0.35 alone),
    consistent with L3H0 preferring far countries (Exp 4 TDA 3, C3).
  - L0H2 alone gives 0.10–0.18. L4H10 gives 0.09–0.16, and 0.20–0.28 in reverse.
  - L1H11 alone has a negative transfer (−0.16 to −0.01): its wrong-run write lowers BOS further.
  - Reverse transfer of all four is 0.45–0.58, larger than forward in three of four cells. The heads' effect is not
    additive with the rest of the run, most strongly at common d = 16.
- **Pairs differ a lot.** At d = 16, F(6) is positive for every pair (0.40–1.41). The four-head G, however, ranges
  from −0.72 (Cairo, common) to +0.87 (Ottawa, rare). It is negative for Cairo in both fillers and for Beijing in
  common. The pooled P-b number is
  an average over heterogeneous pairs, and with 11 fixed pairs it is not a general estimate.

## Caveats

- The same 11 country–capital pairs (and 11 fixed wrong pairings) throughout: row-bootstrap CIs mean consistent over contexts, not general over pairs. The per-pair table shows how much the pairs vary.
- Patching x_l(T) leaves positions τ..T−1 different, so F(l) is what T's stream carries relative to what later layers can still read from those positions; F(l) for l > 6 is measured on fewer layers (max(l, 6)–11), as pre-registered, so the curve mixes a change of site with a change of measured layers.
- P-b patches the four heads' z (equivalently their writes, `hook_result`) at T only; their writes at positions < T still differ between the runs. Heads alone need not add up to the four together (interactions).
- Fractions are ratios of row means; a fraction outside [0, 1] means overshoot (patched beyond the other run) or a push in the opposite direction, not an error.
- TransformerLens with default weight processing; BOS attention is unaffected by the processing (checked in Exp 4/4b against Hugging Face), and no HF cross-check of the patched runs was made here.
