## Caveats

- The same 11 country-capital pairs in every row: a result means consistent across rows (row bootstrap), not general across pairs.
- m is a mean over 100 rows x 11 cells; the 31 trajectory points carry that sampling noise, so H0 / path length of a noisy trajectory partly reflect noise (path/end-to-end is inflated by it).
- D pools d 1-4 (4 points) against d 16-31 (16 points) and divides by the mean norm; it can change with depth through the norm denominator as well as the numerator.
- The wrong-capital and random-final-token conditions are descriptive; no multiplicity correction across them.
- Layer 12 is the state after layer 11 (pre-ln_final).
