## Caveats

- **The same 11 pairs and one derangement throughout** (cell k = capital: own country vs wrong country; Paris: France vs China, Berlin: Germany vs Ukraine, Rome: Italy vs Japan, Madrid: Spain vs Russia, Moscow: Russia vs Italy, Dublin: Ireland vs Spain, Kiev: Ukraine vs Egypt, Tokyo: Japan vs France, Beijing: China vs Canada, Cairo: Egypt vs Ireland, Ottawa: Canada vs Germany). Pooling the 11 cells cancels each country's and each capital's main effect, so a consistent mean δ is a capital × country interaction shared across these pairs relative to this derangement; "beyond the null" means consistent across rows for these pairs, not general.
- **The per-δ sign-flip null is conservative** for S: within a row the country main effects cancel across the 11 cells, so the real mean δ varies less than the flipped one. The row-level null above is the calibrated alternative.
- **R1-c's null is an operational choice.** Flipping single δ's leaves {δ} ∪ {−δ} unchanged, so the literal sign-flip null equals the observed diagram in every draw (p = 1 everywhere). The re-centred null keeps the observed scatter and gives the cloud a sign-flip-sized shift; the projection basis is fitted once on the observed cloud.
- **Rips H0 is single linkage** and chains (Exp 4). On a mirror-symmetric cloud every merge that does not join a set to its own mirror image happens twice at the same scale, so a positive gap means the last merge joins two mirror halves; whether those halves are the ± labels is what the linkage agreement shows.
- **The probe sees 768 features and 1,760 training states per fold.** High accuracy is easy to reach with 11 fixed pairs; LOCO is the stricter test of one shared direction.
- Statistics come from the fp16 arrays of exp5.extract (cast to float64 after loading).
