{\Large\bfseries When do GPT-2's attention heads rest?}\hfill{\footnotesize\color{muted} GPT-2 small · BOS + 32 tokens · common filler shown}\\[2pt]
\fcolorbox{axis}{plane}{\parbox{\dimexpr\linewidth-2\fboxsep-2\fboxrule}{\small\textbf{Idea:} late heads rest on BOS (a no-op) when the context has nothing for the current token to use, and stop when the current token completes it.\\
{\footnotesize Examples below are real rows: \tb\ start token \ \tk{gray} random filler \ \tw{blue} inserted words \ \tq{orange} final token, where attention is measured.}}}

\vspace{3pt}
\exper{\color{cblue}\ding{172}\ \color{black}Experiment 15 · more coherent text $\to$ more resting}{1:00--7:00}
{1 to 31 words of natural text before a random final token.}
{Resting levels off after about 3 words.}
{Fewer random tokens: matched with scrambled words and look-alike random words.}
{\textbf{Wrong:} resting keeps rising, +0.13 / +0.16 at 31 words, even without the last word pair.}
{\ex{8 words}\tb\tk{ir}\tk{ations}\tk{01}\gap{19 random}\tk{--------}\tw{he}\tw{knew}\tw{that}\tw{tomorrow}\tw{the}\tw{weather}\tw{would}\tw{be}\tq{count}\newline
 \ex{31 words}\tb\tw{sand}\tw{as}\tw{the}\tw{sun}\tw{went}\tw{down}\gap{21 words}\tw{the}\tw{weather}\tw{would}\tw{be}\tq{count}}
{Most heads already rest on BOS; the change maps turn redder as the text gets longer.}

\exper{\color{cblue}\ding{173}\ \color{black}Experiments 9--10 · completion releases it}{7:00--13:00}
{Capital as the final token, with its country 1 to 31 positions earlier.}
{A correct capital pulls late heads off BOS.}
{Final-token identity: each capital used once correctly and ten times wrongly.}
{\textbf{Right:} $-0.018$ / $-0.034$ in 11/11 countries, from every position; placebo 0 of 496.}
{\ex{correct}\tb\tk{she}\tk{sc}\tk{sa}\gap{27 random}\tw{France}\tq{Paris}\quad
 \ex{wrong}\tb\tk{she}\gap{29 random}\tw{Japan}\tq{Paris}\quad
 \ex{far}\tb\tw{France}\tk{sc}\tk{sa}\gap{27 random}\tk{dis}\tq{Paris}}
{Late heads turn blue (less resting) for correct capitals, whether the country is adjacent or far.}
{meeting_map_2.png}

\exper{\color{cblue}\ding{174}\ \color{black}Experiment 8 · truth alone does not}{13:00--19:00}
{True vs false country--capital pair before a random final token.}
{A true pair raises resting more than a false one.}
{Token identity: full country $\times$ capital grid; GPT-2 knows all 11 capitals.}
{\textbf{Wrong:} true $\approx$ false ($-0.001$ / $-0.004$); any real pair raises resting (+0.013 / +0.05).}
{\ex{true}\tb\tk{she}\tk{sc}\tk{sa}\gap{26 random}\tw{France}\tw{Paris}\tq{ious}\qquad
 \ex{false}\tb\tk{she}\tk{sc}\tk{sa}\gap{26 random}\tw{Japan}\tw{Paris}\tq{ious}}
{Correct and wrong pairs give the same red change vs no pair.}
{meeting_map_3.png}
